About Me

Nadia holds a Bachelor's degree in Management Information Systems, which underpins her structured, data-driven approach to problem solving. As a Senior Associate and GIS Data Analyst, she has been part of the Fernridge team since 2011 - 15 years and counting.

Over that time she has worked on all types of retail and shopping centre demographic feasibility and consumer insights studies, presenting her findings in clear, concise reports. She has taken a leading role in designing and building business systems and BI dashboards for both internal and external users. She also regularly engages with stakeholders to understand their needs and identify opportunities for insight that inform their decision-making.
